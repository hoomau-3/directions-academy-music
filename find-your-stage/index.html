Find Your Stage

Pick an age. Hear the stage.

Every stage of life asks a different question. Choose an age below to find the stage it belongs to, the song written for it, and a free two-page quick guide.

Remember: stages, not ages. The ages are only a guide. We revisit old questions all through our lives.

    Hear Trust These Arms →Stage 1 Quick Guide (PDF)

  2. Ages 18-24 months to 3 years

    Stage 2: Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt

    Can I stand on my own two feet?

    Hear Safe to Make a Little Mess →Stage 2 Quick Guide (PDF)

  3. Ages 3 to 5 years

    Stage 3: Initiative vs. Guilt

    Do I dare turn my ideas into action?

    Hear Allowed to Want →Stage 3 Quick Guide (PDF)

  4. Ages 6 to 11 years

    Stage 4: Industry vs. Inferiority

    Do my efforts matter?

    Hear Build It Crooked →Stage 4 Quick Guide (PDF)
