What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · Knowing who you do it for

The busy middle years can fill a life with things: a faster car, a bigger title, a longer flight away from home.

None of it makes the quiet go away.

Then a child calls, small-voiced, asking which way to go, and the question changes. Not what am I doing all this for, but who.

What fills a life is the hand you hold and the words you choose to say.

It is never too late to turn back toward the people you love.

To think about

  • Who are you really doing it all for?
  • What have you been buying, or chasing, to make the quiet go away?
  • When did a call from someone you love change what mattered most?
