Parenting by Design

Songs for parents, and for anyone who may become one.

Nobody starts parenting with a blank page. Long before we teach our children to trust, listen, love, or apologize, we are drawing from lessons we learned ourselves.

These songs explore the habits, fears, rules, blessings, and wounds that travel from one generation to the next. Some are gifts. Some are burdens. Most are a little of both.

The moment we become parents, we begin deciding what continues and what changes.

Because every parent eventually discovers that the child in front of them is not the only child in the room.

A gentle note

These songs come from real lives, and some of them are heavy. They touch on childhood wounds, anger, exhaustion, and patterns passed from one generation to the next.

Listen at your own pace. Pause when you need to.
