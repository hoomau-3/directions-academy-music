What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · The default setting

Nobody chooses their first parenting style. It arrives long before the first child does, built from the voices, habits, and reactions that filled childhood. Most of the time, it runs without permission.

That is not failure. It is how families pass themselves from one generation to the next.

The turning point is not perfection. It is awareness.

You cannot change the code you have never seen.

To think about

  • What is one moment lately when you heard your own parents in your voice?
  • Which parts of your upbringing deserve to continue, and which deserve to end?
  • How do you catch the autopilot before it starts flying the plane?
