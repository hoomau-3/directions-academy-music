What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · A blessing for the road

This is a blessing, not a promise of an easy road.

It wishes a child enough trials to grow strong, enough sorrow to stay human, enough failure to stay humble, and enough success to keep reaching.

No parent can spare a child every hard day.

What we can hope is that they meet those days with courage, kindness, and the strength to rise again.

To think about

  • What do you wish for the people you love: an easy life, or enough?
  • Which hard season in your life made you stronger, or kinder?
  • What blessing would you like to say out loud to your child?
