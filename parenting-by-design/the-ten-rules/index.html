What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · Learning to be human

None of us arrive finished. Life is the classroom, and every day is a lesson begun.

Mistakes are not proof of failure. They are roads gone wrong that can still lead somewhere good.

The same lesson often returns in different forms until we are ready to learn it.

Children do not need perfect parents.

They need parents who are still willing to learn.

To think about

  • What lesson keeps coming back to you in different clothes?
  • What mistake turned out to be a doorway?
  • What would change if you saw yourself as someone still becoming?
