What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · Learning to be human

None of us arrive finished. Life is the classroom, and every day is a lesson begun. Mistakes are not proof of failure, only roads gone wrong that can still lead somewhere good. The same storm keeps coming back, in different clothes, until we learn what it came to teach. Children learn this best from parents who are still learning too.

To think about

  • What lesson keeps coming back to you in different clothes?
  • What mistake turned out to be a doorway?
  • What would change if you saw yourself as someone still becoming?
