What this song teaches

Parenting by Design · Breaking the chain

What was done to us often gets done through us, until someone turns and faces it. Families pass down wounds, wisdom, fears, and habits, sometimes without meaning to.

Stopping a pattern does not require a perfect childhood.

It requires one person willing to see clearly, grieve honestly, and choose differently.

That work costs something.

It also becomes a gift to people you may never meet.

To think about

  • What pattern in your family would you like to stop with you?
  • What does it cost to be the one who stops it?
  • Who can stand beside you while you do?
