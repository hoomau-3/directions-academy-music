Because of You

Songs for the people who shaped me.

Every life is touched by a few people who leave footprints on our hearts so deep that we carry them forever.

Some teach us how to think.

Some teach us how to love.

Some walk beside us for a season.

Some for a lifetime.

And sometimes we don't fully understand what they meant to us until years later.

These songs are my attempt to say thank you.

So far, there are two.

One for my cousin Brent, whose intellect, curiosity, and friendship expanded my world every time we were together.

One for my wife Eileen, whose patience, love, and steady presence helped me become a better man than I could ever have become alone.

More songs will follow.

Because the older I get, the more I realize that none of us become who we are by ourselves.

If there is someone who shaped your life, tell them what they mean to you while you still can.

And if you no longer can, tell their story.

That's what these songs are for.
