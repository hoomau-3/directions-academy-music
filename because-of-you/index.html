Because of You

Songs for the people who shaped me.

Every life is touched by a few people who leave footprints on our hearts so deep we carry them forever.

Some teach us how to think.

Some teach us how to love.

Some walk beside us for a season.

Some for a lifetime.

And sometimes we do not understand what they gave us until years later.

These songs are my attempt to say thank you.

So far, there are three.

One is for my cousin Brent, whose intellect, curiosity, and friendship expanded my world every time we were together.

One is for my wife Eileen, whose patience, love, and steady presence helped me become a better man than I could ever have become alone.

And one is for The Country Rebels, the last group I played with: Tony, George and Von, who shared the stage, the late-night breakfasts at Denny's, and my love of music.

More songs will follow.

Because the older I get, the more I realize that none of us become who we are by ourselves. We are all, in ways seen and unseen, the work of other hearts.

If there is someone who shaped your life, tell them what they mean to you while you still can.

And if you no longer can, tell their story.

Sometimes gratitude becomes a conversation. Sometimes it becomes a song.
