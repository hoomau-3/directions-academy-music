What this song holds

Becoming · Song 2 of 12

This is a song about permission. The girl in this story is learning that changing your mind is not failure. Dreams shift. Questions appear. The future refuses to arrive on schedule. The adults who love her cannot walk the road for her, but they can leave the light on while she finds her way. Sometimes becoming who you are begins by giving yourself permission not to know yet.

To think about

  • Who decided that growth should happen on someone else's timeline?
  • What part of you is still becoming something new?
  • When you need room to grow, what helps you still feel loved?
