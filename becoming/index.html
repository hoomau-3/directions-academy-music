Becoming

One girl. Twelve songs. One story, told in order.

She starts out unseen and unheard, left on read by the people she needed most. Song by song she learns to want things, to push back, to stand on her own, and to open a door. Then she looks back with compassion at who she was. Her story is made from real stories shared by former students.

How to listen

Start with song 1 and go in order. Each song builds on the one before it. Every song page has the video and the full lyrics. Songs are released on Sundays, so some are still on their way.
