Becoming

One girl. Twelve songs. One story told in order.

She keeps checking for a reply that never comes. Wonders if she's behind. Falls in love. Makes mistakes. Becomes a mother. Learns who stays, who leaves, and what to do with both.

Each song is a chapter in her journey from needing other people to tell her who she is to finally feeling at home in her own skin.

If you've ever felt invisible, unwanted, misunderstood, left behind, or not enough, part of this story may feel familiar.

Inspired by real experiences shared by former students, Becoming is ultimately about one question:

What if the person you've been searching for all along is you?

A gentle note

These songs are inspired by real experiences and touch on loneliness, body image, first love, heartbreak, being left behind, unexpected motherhood, and finding your own worth.

Listen at your own pace. Pause when you need to.

If a song hits close to home, you are not alone.

How to listen

Start with song 1 and follow her story in order. Each song picks up where the last one left off, revealing another part of who she is and who she is becoming. Every song page includes the video and full lyrics, inviting you to experience the story through both music and words.
