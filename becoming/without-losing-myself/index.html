What this song holds

Becoming · Song 4 of 12

This is a song about boundaries. The girl in this story has worked hard to find her own voice, and she does not want to lose it inside a relationship. She is discovering that healthy love does not require self-erasure. Two people can choose each other without giving up who they are. Being known is not the same as being absorbed. Sometimes real love makes room for both people to grow.

To think about

  • Where have you felt pressure to become less of yourself to belong?
  • What part of yourself do you hope never has to change to be loved?
  • What helps you stay yourself when you care deeply about someone?
