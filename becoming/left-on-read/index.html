What this song holds

Becoming · Song 1 of 12

This is a song about self-worth. The girl in this story has learned to measure herself through other people's eyes. An unanswered text feels like a verdict. A passing glance feels like proof. But this song begins when she stops asking strangers to decide her value. No one comes to rescue her. Sometimes freedom begins when we stop handing other people the ruler.

To think about

  • Where are you still letting other people decide what you're worth?
  • Whose voice still carries more weight than your own?
  • What is one small way you could choose your own voice today?
