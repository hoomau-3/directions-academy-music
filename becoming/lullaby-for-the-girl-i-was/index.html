What this song holds

Becoming · Song 12 of 12

This is a song about coming home. The woman in this story returns to the girl she used to be and offers the kindness she once needed. No more fixing. No more proving. No more running. This song is a lullaby for the child who held on through every hard chapter. You were never too much. You were never not enough. Sometimes healing ends when we become the safe place we spent our lives searching for.

To think about

  • What does the younger you most need to hear today?
  • What strength did the younger you have that you still rely on today?
  • What part of you is finally ready to rest?
