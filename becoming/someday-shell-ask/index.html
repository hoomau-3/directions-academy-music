What this song holds

Becoming · Song 10 of 12

This is a song about honesty. The mother in this story knows a day is coming when her daughter will ask hard questions. She decides to tell the whole truth, not the polished version. The story includes pain, mistakes, and fear, but it also includes what came next. This song is about trusting the truth to carry its own meaning. Shame grows in silence. Sometimes healing begins when the whole story is finally told.

To think about

  • What truth became easier to carry once it was spoken aloud?
  • What truth would become easier to hear if love spoke first?
  • What part of your story is still waiting for a new ending?
