What this song holds

Becoming · Song 7 of 12

This is a song about belonging. The girl in this story had spent years feeling like a mistake waiting to be confirmed. Then one teacher opened a door and saw more than a statistic. She saw a student, a voice, and someone worth investing in. Sometimes a single invitation changes a life. Most rescues are not dramatic. They look like a door held open and people refusing to look away.

To think about

  • Who saw something in you before you could see it yourself?
  • What possibility have you already decided is closed to you?
  • Who might need someone to believe in them the way you were?
