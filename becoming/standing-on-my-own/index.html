What this song holds

Becoming · Song 6 of 12

This is a song about refusing a label. The girl in this story became a mother young, and people were quick to decide what her future would be. They did not see the long nights, hard choices, or strength it took to keep going. This song begins the day she stops letting others define her story. Surviving matters. Sometimes standing on your own begins with refusing to be reduced to your hardest chapter.

To think about

  • Where have you been carrying blame that was never yours to hold?
  • What would it look like to trust your own strength this week?
  • If she were beside you tonight, what would you need to hear?
