What this song holds

Becoming · Song 5 of 12

This is a song about a promise. Not a promise to be perfect, but a promise to be intentional. The girl in this story is sixteen, pregnant, and determined that things will be different for her child. She cannot change where she came from or pretend her wounds are healed. Instead, she chooses a promise she can keep and has the courage to ask for help. Sometimes breaking a cycle begins with a decision made every day.

To think about

  • What part of your story do you hope ends with you?
  • What promise would matter most if you kept it every day?
  • Who reminds you that you don't have to carry everything alone?
