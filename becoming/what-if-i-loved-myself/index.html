What this song holds

Becoming · Song 3 of 12

This is a song about self-compassion. The girl in this story has spent years speaking to herself with a harshness she would never use with someone she loves. Slowly, she begins to ask a different question: would I say this to a friend? The voice in her head did not appear on its own, and it does not change overnight. Sometimes healing begins one kinder thought at a time.

To think about

  • Who first taught you to speak to yourself that way?
  • What would you say to yourself if you cared for yourself deeply?
  • What is one thing you would thank yourself for today?
