Listen To Your Heart

For HimFor Her

Everybody has an opinion about your heart. Only one voice is yours.

Three songs, each told twice: once for him and once for her.

A boy chases someone who left. A girl learns to choose herself. Both are told they're too young to know what love is. Friends, parents, and strangers all have advice. Through first love, heartbreak, fear, hope, and healing, they discover the same truth: advice can guide you, but your heart gets the final vote.

Because growing up isn't finding all the answers. It's learning to trust yourself when nobody else can tell you what the right answer is.

A gentle note

These songs come from real lives, and some of them are heavy. They touch on heartbreak, family pressure, fear, and letting someone go.

Listen at your own pace. Pause when you need to.
