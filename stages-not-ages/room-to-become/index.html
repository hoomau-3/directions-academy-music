What this song teaches

Stage 5: Identity vs. Role Confusion · adolescence

Teenagers try on identities the way they try on clothes, and the fitting room is often a mess. That is the work of adolescence: answering "Who am I?" through exploration and experience. The adults' job is to hold the walls steady while the trying-on happens, not to pick the outfit. The goal is not raising a copy of yourself. The goal is giving a young person enough room to become themselves.

To think about

  • Which of my teen's experiments truly worry me, and which are simply different from my own?
  • Can my teen become someone different from me and still stay connected to me?
  • Who gave me room to become myself, and who expected me to be someone else?
