What this song teaches

Stage 5: Identity vs. Role Confusion · adolescence

Teenagers try on selves the way they try on clothes, and the fitting room is a mess. That is the job of adolescence: to answer “who am I?” by testing. The adults' job is to hold the walls steady while the trying-on happens, not to pick the outfit.

To think about

  • Which of my teen's experiments actually worry me, and which just surprise me?
  • Can my child be different from me and still be close to me?
  • Who gave me room to become, and who did not?
