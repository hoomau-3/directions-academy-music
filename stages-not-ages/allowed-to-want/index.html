Stage 3: Initiative vs. Guilt

Allowed to Want

I have ideas. Can I try things?

This song is for both of you.

Read the lyrics together, print them if you like, then play the song together.

Watch on YouTube

Lyrics

Verse 1

You stacked the cushions by the window
With a blanket for a sail
A wooden spoon became a compass
And a cardboard box a rail
I saw the tower lean and tremble
I saw your careful hands begin
You looked at me as if to ask me
If the world would let you in

Pre-Chorus

Some things are too high to climb
Some roads must wait another time
But wanting is not wrong, my dear
I’m right beside you, I am here

Chorus

You are allowed to want the thing
To reach for what you see
To draw a door where there’s a wall
And wonder what could be
If I say, “Not this way today,”
It’s not your heart I’m turning from
The limit guards the hands you have
Your wanting still belongs

Verse 2

You carried pebbles to the garden
For a place you called a town
Gave the smallest one a rooftop
When the paper houses fell down
Then you spilled the jar of water
Trying to make a river run
Your eyes filled up with sudden worry
As if wishing caused the mess you’d done

Pre-Chorus

The floor can wait for towels tonight
We’ll make the broken edges right
A hard result is not your name
And trying is not something to blame

Chorus

You are allowed to want the thing
To reach for what you see
To ask a thousand hows and whys
And test what you can be
If I say, “That could hurt you now,”
It’s not your heart I’m turning from
The limit guards the hands you have
Your wanting still belongs

Bridge

There will be days the plan goes crooked
Days the answer isn’t clear
I won’t make your bright ideas
Something you should hide from fear
Bring me every wild beginning
Bring the ones that fall apart
I will teach you where the edges are
Without dimming what’s in your heart

Final Chorus

You are allowed to want the thing
To reach for what you see
To build a little doorway wide
Enough for you and me
And if the world says, “Wait awhile,”
We’ll find another way along
The limit names the choice we make
It never names you wrong
You are allowed to want the thing
And I’ll help you learn to start
Your hands can shape a kinder world
With your brave and wondering heart

Outro

The cushions wait beside the window
Your compass points ahead
I’ll hold the map, you choose the journey
And tuck you safely into bed
