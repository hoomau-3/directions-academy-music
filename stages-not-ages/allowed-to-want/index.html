What this song teaches

Stage 3: Initiative vs. Guilt · approx. 3 to 5 years

Around age three, children stop simply reacting to the world and start making plans for it: games, schemes, questions, big ideas. When wanting is welcomed, a child learns their ideas are worth having. When every idea is swatted down, the wanting itself starts to feel wrong, and that feeling can follow a person for decades.

To think about

  • How do I answer the ideas that arrive at the worst possible moment?
  • Can my child want something I would never choose?
  • What did I learn, growing up, about asking for what I wanted?
