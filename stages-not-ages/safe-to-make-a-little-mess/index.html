Stage 2: Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt · approx. 18-24 months to 3 years

Somewhere around eighteen months, a child discovers two mighty words: no, and me do it. That is not defiance. It is the first draft of independence. A child who is allowed to try, spill, and try again learns I can. A child who is shamed for trying learns to doubt their own hands.

To think about

  • Where could I let my child try, even though it is slower and messier?
  • What is the difference between keeping my child safe and keeping my child stopped?
  • How was my own “me do it” answered when I was small?
