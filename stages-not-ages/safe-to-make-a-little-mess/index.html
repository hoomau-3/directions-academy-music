What this song teaches

Stage 2: Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt · approx. 18-24 months to 3 years

Somewhere around eighteen months, a child discovers two mighty words: "No" and "Me do it." That is not defiance. It is the first draft of independence. A child who is allowed to try, spill, wobble, and try again learns, "I can." A child who is shamed for trying begins to doubt their own abilities. The goal is not getting it right the first time. The goal is having the courage to try.

To think about

  • Where could I let my child try, even if it takes longer and gets messier?
  • Am I protecting my child, or protecting them from growing?
  • Who made it safe for me to say, "I can do it myself"?
