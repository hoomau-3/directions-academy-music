What this song teaches

Stage 1: Trust vs. Mistrust · approx. birth to 18-24 months

A baby cannot ask for trust in words. They ask by crying, reaching, and settling into loving arms. Each time comfort comes, the same lesson is repeated: people can be counted on. That lesson becomes the foundation for everything that follows. Not perfectly, just reliably. The parent in this song misses a beat, loses her temper, and still comes back. In the end, that is what trust asks of us.

To think about

  • What is my baby learning about the world each time I comfort them?
  • Who showed me that people can be trusted, especially when life was hard?
  • What helps me come back with love on the nights when nothing seems to help?
