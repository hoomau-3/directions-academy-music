What this song teaches

Stage 1: Trust vs. Mistrust · approx. birth to 18-24 months

A baby cannot ask for trust in words. They ask by crying, reaching, and settling, and every time comfort comes, a lesson lands: people can be counted on. That lesson becomes the floor the rest of life is built on. Not perfectly, just reliably. The parent in this song misses a beat, loses her temper, and still comes back. That is the whole assignment.

To think about

  • When my baby cries, what are they learning about asking for help?
  • Who taught me whether people can be counted on?
  • What helps me stay steady on the nights when nothing soothes?
