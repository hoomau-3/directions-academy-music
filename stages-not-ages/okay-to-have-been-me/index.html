Stage 8: Integrity vs. Despair

Okay to Have Been Me

Is it okay to have been me?

This song is for you, and for someone who loves you.

If someone you love is in these years, sit with them. Read this page aloud, then listen to the song together.

Watch on YouTube

Lyrics

Verse 1

I count the years by the marks on the kitchen door
By the names on the stones I still kneel before
There were roads I took that I would not take again
And a few hard truths I was too proud to let in

Pre-Chorus

But the hand that trembled still held on
The wrong turns taught me where I belonged

Chorus

Is it okay to have been me?
With the good and the damage no one else could see
If I could mend every broken thread
Would I lose the life those broken moments made?
I am not clean, I am not free
But the truth has made a home in me
When the last door opens quietly
I hope I can say: it was okay to have been me

Verse 2

I see my children in the way they stand
Not asking the future to fit their plans
If I taught them courage, it was not by being brave
I just kept walking through the fear I could not name

Pre-Chorus

They learned from the nights I did not run
That death is not the opposite of love

Chorus

Is it okay to have been me?
With the years I threw away and the ones that carried me
If I could silence every word I said
Would I erase the roads that brought them to my bed?
I am not clean, I am not free
But the truth has made a home in me
When the last door opens quietly
I hope I can say: it was okay to have been me

Bridge

Wisdom is not a spotless page
Or a clever answer polished bright with age
It is a steady hand when the room goes dark
A little earned light, a stubborn spark
The scar is proof the skin survived
The regret is proof I was alive

Guitar Solo

Final Chorus

Is it okay to have been me?
With the love I failed to give and the grace that carried me
Every sorrow has a place
In the shape of this unfinished, ordinary grace
I am not clean, I am not free
But I know what these years have made of me
When the last door opens quietly
I will not hide from who I had to be

Outro

Let the children find their own way home
Let them meet the dark and know they are not alone

A life with some regret, and enough love to see it through
Is it okay to have been me?
