What this song teaches

Stage 8: Integrity vs. Despair · later adulthood

Looking back on a whole life, everyone meets the same gentle question: is it okay to have been me? Integrity is not a perfect record. It is making peace with the record that exists: the choices, the mistakes, and the love, all of it yours. The goal is not to erase regret. The goal is to see the whole story clearly and still be able to say, "It was okay to have been me."

To think about

  • Which chapters of my life have I made peace with, and which still ache?
  • What would I say to my younger self, with kindness?
  • What do I want the people I love to know while I can still tell them?
