Stage 9: Very Old Age

Tell the Family I Am Okay

A message from someone very old to the people who love them.

This song is for someone you love in these years, and for the people who love them.

Sit with them. Read this page aloud, then listen to the song together.

Watch on YouTube

Verse 1

I used to reach the high shelf
And carry every chair
I knew the roads by memory
Could fix most anything there
Now the kettle waits for morning
And my hands take longer still
But I know the sound of your footsteps
And I know the window’s hill

Pre-Chorus

I may need your arm beside me
I may rest before we go
That is not a door closing
It’s a slower way to know

Chorus

Tell the family I am okay
I’m learning what to keep

The friend who sits with me
I cannot give my yes away
To every passing thing
But where I place the little left
Still has a meaning

And bless the help it’s worth
