Stages Not Ages

Nine songs, one for each stage of a human life, from a baby learning to trust the arms that hold her to the last chapter of very old age.

The stages come from the work of Erik Erikson. Each one has its own big question, and each song lives inside that question. The name says it all: stages, not ages. We do not move through them on a schedule. Some of us revisit a stage many times.

The ages shown are approximate. Experts draw the lines in different places, and people grow at their own pace.

How to listen

You can start at Stage 1 and walk the whole road in order. Or you can start with the stage you are in right now, then go back to the beginning.

If you are a parent, listen to the stage your child is in, and then to your own. Each song tells you what that stage needs, and what it feels like from the inside.

Every song has its own page with the video and the full lyrics.

The songs

  1. Trust These ArmsApprox. birth to 18-24 months
    Stage 1: Trust vs. Mistrust. Is the world safe?
  2. Safe to Make a Little MessApprox. 18-24 months to 3 years
    Stage 2: Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt. Can I do it myself?
  3. Allowed to WantApprox. 3 to 5 years
    Stage 3: Initiative vs. Guilt. I have ideas. Can I try things?
  4. Build It CrookedApprox. 6 to 11 years
    Stage 4: Industry vs. Inferiority. Do my efforts count?
  5. Room to BecomeApprox. 12 to 18-20 years
    Stage 5: Identity vs. Role Confusion. Who am I becoming?
  6. Without Losing MyselfApprox. 18 to 40 years
    Stage 6: Intimacy vs. Isolation. Can you be close to someone without losing yourself?
  7. Learning to CareApprox. 40 to 65 years
    Stage 7: Generativity vs. Stagnation. Who am I doing all of this for?
  8. Okay to Have Been MeApprox. 65 to 80 years
    Stage 8: Integrity vs. Despair. Is it okay to have been me?
  9. Tell the Family I Am OkayApprox. 80 and up
    Stage 9: Very Old Age. A message from someone very old to the people who love them.
