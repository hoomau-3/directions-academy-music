Stage 7: Generativity vs. Stagnation · approx. 40 to 65

The middle years hand us calendars, deadlines, and titles, and they can still leave a hollow feeling at the end of a full day. That feeling is a signal, not a failure. Something in us wants our days to land in other people: children, students, neighbors, anyone we help grow.

To think about

  • Who receives my days?
  • What am I building that will outlast my job title?
  • Where could caring replace accumulating this year?
