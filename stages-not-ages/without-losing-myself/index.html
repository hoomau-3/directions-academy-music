What this song teaches

Stage 6: Intimacy vs. Isolation · young adulthood

Real closeness asks us to be known, not performed. That is only possible for a person who still has a self to bring. Keeping a suitcase packed by the door feels safe, but it keeps love at arm's length too. The work of this stage is learning to stay, and to stay yourself. Real intimacy does not ask us to disappear. It asks us to bring our whole selves into the relationship.

To think about

  • What am I afraid someone would see if I let them know me completely?
  • Where do I hide parts of myself in order to be loved?
  • What would it look like to stay close and stay myself?
