Lyrics

Verse 1

You lined up blocks beside the kitchen chair
One leaned left, but you left it there
Your little hands kept trying different ways
While morning poured gold across the plates

You frowned when the tower tumbled down
Then found the tallest piece on the ground
I watched you study the pieces in your palm
And build another world before the dawn

Pre-Chorus

You don’t have to win the room
You don’t have to know too soon
Every stumble leaves a clue
And I believe in what you do

Chorus

Are you actually good at anything?
You ask with your eyes when the hard days sting
Yes, my love, though not because you never fall
You keep reaching back for one more try, that’s all
The world will measure what it can see
But your brave work is enough for me

Verse 2

At six, your letters wandered off the line
You hid the page and said, “That isn’t mine”
Then Tuesday brought a word you couldn’t read
You sounded it out, one careful seed

The boy beside you finished first again
You folded up your paper like a tent
I said, “Look how far your pencil came today”
And you looked down, then slowly looked my way

Pre-Chorus

You don’t have to guard a name
You don’t have to fear the game
Being new is not defeat
Learning grows beneath your feet

Chorus

Are you actually good at anything?
You ask when somebody else gets wings
Yes, my love, though not because you know it all
You keep reaching back for one more try, that’s all
The world will measure what it can see
But your brave work is enough for me

Bridge

One day you’ll choose a door I cannot open
Carry questions like a pocketful of tokens
Someone may praise the shine and miss the strain
Someone may laugh before you try again
Remember this: the hands that start unsure
Can learn the shape of something difficult and pure

Final Chorus

Are you actually good at anything?
Come here, let me answer everything
You are good at staying with the work awhile
Good at finding one small reason to smile
Good at saying, “I don’t know, but I can learn”
Good at letting every wrong turn take its turn
The world will measure what it can see
But your growing heart is enough for me

Outro

So build it crooked, read it slow
Let the unfinished pieces show
I’ll be here when the tower falls
Cheering for the hands that build it all
