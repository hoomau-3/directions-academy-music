Privacy

In plain words: this site counts visits, never people.

What we collect

A simple count of visits, through Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not identify anyone. We can see that a page was visited, never who visited it.

Beyond that, nothing. This site has no accounts, no log-ins, and no forms of its own. We do not use advertising. We cannot see who you are, and we do not want to.

The music player

Songs play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. Until you press play, YouTube loads nothing. Once you press play, YouTube (part of Google) delivers the video and may use cookies under its own privacy policy.

The newsletter box

The sign-up box for Developing the Healthy Mind is provided by Substack. If you enter your email address there, it goes to Substack, not to this site, and is handled under Substack's privacy policy. You can unsubscribe with one click from any issue.

The send button

The "Send this song to someone" button works entirely on your own device. We never see who you send a song to, or that you sent one at all.

Links to other sites

Pages link to YouTube, Spotify, and Substack. Once you leave this site, their privacy policies apply.
