Privacy

In plain words: this site collects nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no log-ins, no forms of its own, and it sets no cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking of any kind. We cannot see who you are, and we do not want to.

The music player

Songs play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. Until you press play, YouTube loads nothing. When you press play, YouTube (part of Google) delivers the video and may use cookies under its own privacy policy.

The newsletter box

The sign-up box for Developing the Healthy Mind is provided by Substack. If you type your email address there, it goes to Substack, not to this site, and is handled under Substack's privacy policy. You can unsubscribe with one click in any issue.

The send button

The "Send this song to someone" button works entirely on your own device. We never see who you send a song to, or that you sent one at all.

Links to other sites

Pages link to YouTube, Spotify, and Substack. Each has its own privacy policy once you are there.
