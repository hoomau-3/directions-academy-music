How the music was made

I can no longer sing, and even when I could, I was often off-key.

I began using computers to develop musical ideas in the early 1990s, when MIDI, the new Musical Instrument Digital Interface, let me play every instrument from a keyboard. I created a dozen songs, though recording them to analog was cumbersome; digital technology improved the process later, but it was still unwieldy.

At least my band members never argued, I was the entire band.

Today, AI fulfills that early dream by turning my musical sketches into fully produced songs. I’m grateful it arrived in my lifetime.
