Leave a Little Light Behind · Song 3 of 3

Sunday afternoons at the house: kids in the yard, Mama at the stove, always room for one more chair. The memories matter, but not because the past was perfect. They remind us of something easy to forget: the stars still shine, the ocean still heals, and kindness still matters. Maybe the best we can do is leave a little light behind, and trust the people we love will find it when they need it.

To think about

  • What did a slow Sunday look like in your family?
  • What beauty around you have you stopped noticing?
  • When will you share a meal and look someone in the eye?
