Leave a Little Light Behind · Song 2 of 3

A man whose wife is gone finds her everywhere: in morning light at the window, in familiar rooms, in a laugh he still remembers. Some days the memories fade like a path beneath the snow. Then a friend tells a story, or sings the part he forgot, and she is close again. The people we love go on living in the people they touched.

To think about

  • Whose story do you carry, and who still tells it with you?
  • When has someone else's memory brought a loved one back to you?
  • What kindness would you like to keep growing in others after you?
