About the Music

AiNa Lyfe is a collection of songs, stories, and small life lessons about growing up, raising children, finding our place, and navigating life's many seasons.

Some albums tell a story from beginning to end. Others explore a single idea, relationship, or stage of life. All are written with compassion, curiosity, and hope.

About the Author

David has worn a few hats over the years: teacher, researcher, musician, lyricist, husband, father, and grandfather. He taught Human Development to college students, worked in Army medical research, & served as Director of the Research Corporation of the University of Hawaiʻi in Hilo.

Through it all, one thread remained constant: a fascination with how people grow, learn, and find their place in the world. Those lessons eventually found their way into these songs.

David lives in Hilo, Hawaiʻi, and writes Developing the Healthy Mind on Substack. You can reach him at hello@directions-academy.com.

About the Use of AI

David has been making music for a long time. In the 1990s he worked with MIDI, recording each instrument one track at a time. He witnessed the transition from analog recording to digital production and embraced every new tool along the way.

For him, AI is simply the next step on that journey, and a remarkable one. It helps transform an idea into a finished song faster than ever before, making it possible to share more stories with more people.

The voices and music are created with AI tools. The words, stories, lessons, and creative decisions are David's. That's why every song is credited: Words by David. Voices and creative partnership with AI.

How to Listen

Choose a song, or choose an album. Some albums tell a story from beginning to end, while others can be explored in any order. Follow your curiosity.

Each song has its own page with the video and full lyrics, so you can listen, watch, or read along. Headphones help.

If a song brings up something heavy, it's okay to pause and sit with it, or talk with someone you trust.

And if a song moves you, share it with someone who might need it.
