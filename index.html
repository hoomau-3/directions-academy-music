If you're wondering why your child suddenly changed, why a strategy that worked yesterday no longer works today, or how to help them become everything they were meant to be, start by finding their current stage.

Few experiences challenge a parent quite like the teenage years.

The child who once depended on you now questions you. The young person you're trying to guide is trying just as hard to discover who they are.

Stage 5 explores the developmental task of adolescence: forming an identity that's truly their own.

Through music, stories, and insights, discover why conflict often increases, independence becomes essential, and your relationship may be more important than ever.

Explore Stage 5 and hear the journey through the eyes of both parent and teen.

Being a teenager isn't easy.

You're figuring out who you are while the people who love you are trying to figure out who you're becoming.

The songs in Becoming speak directly to the teenage experience, while Stage 5 helps parents understand why this season of life can feel confusing for everyone involved.

Maybe neither side is completely wrong.

Maybe you're both growing.

I'm Falling in Love and Finding MyselfBuilding a life together without losing the person you were meant to become. →
Read more

Love invites us into one of life's great challenges.

How do two people become "us" while remaining fully themselves?

Stage 6 explores the developmental journey of Intimacy versus Isolation. It is about learning to love deeply, commit fully, and build a shared identity with a partner without losing your own identity in the process.

The journey is captured in Song Six of Stages Not Ages: Without Losing Myself.

Whether you're dating, engaged, newly married, or simply wondering what healthy love looks like, this stage offers insights for one of life's most important decisions.
