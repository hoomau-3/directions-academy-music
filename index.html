About the Music

AiNa Lyfe makes songs that each carry one small life lesson. The songs come in albums, and each album is a story you can follow from the first song to the last.

They are written for teenagers, and for the adults who love them. The stories are told with care, and without blame.

Please do not skip around the first time. Start at song 1 and let the story build.

About the Author

David has worn a few hats. He is a retired teacher who taught Human Development to college students. He is retired from the Army, where he worked in medical research. He is retired from the Research Corporation of the University of Hawaii, where he was the Director on Hawai'i Island.

Through all of it, he has been a musician and lyricist since "small kid time." He lives in Hilo, Hawaii, and also writes Developing the Healthy Mind on Substack.

You can write to him at ikemanao808@gmail.com.

About the Use of AI

David has been making music a long time. In the nineties he worked with MIDI and played the entire band himself, one track at a time. He went from analog recording to digital, and kept learning at every step.

AI is the next step on that road, and he calls it a magical gift. It takes a song from the idea to the full production version in a fraction of the time. It is the best partner he has ever worked with.

The voices and the music are made with AI tools. The words, the stories, and every decision about what to keep are David's. That is why every song says: Words by David, voices by AI.

We say it plainly because you deserve to know what you are listening to. The Becoming album is built from real stories shared by former students, told with care.

How to Listen
  • Choose an album and start at song 1. Go in order.
  • Take one song at a time. Headphones help.
  • Each song has its own page with the video and the full lyrics, so you can read along.
  • If a song brings up something heavy, it is okay to pause. Talk with someone you trust.
  • When a song moves you, share it with a teenager, or with someone who loves one.
