The story behind the music

This piece feels like being carried effortlessly by a story.

Woodwinds, strings, and gentle orchestral textures create the sensation of floating rather than traveling. Instead of rushing toward a destination, the music invites me to surrender to wonder and allow the journey to unfold.

What the music is saying

Wind Rider is graceful and elegant, like a white seabird riding the trade winds above a palm-lined coast. The music never pushes. It floats, and lets the wind tell the story.

Style: Instrumental Storytelling
Feel: Elegant, Graceful, Transportive

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
