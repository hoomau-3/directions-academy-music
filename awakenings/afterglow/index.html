The story behind the music

This is the final chapter of the journey.

Whenever I hear it, I picture the closing credits of a great film. The story has ended, yet no one is quite ready to leave. Gentle woodwinds and strings build toward a heartfelt climax before settling into reflection and peace.

It is not a song about endings.

It is a song about what remains after something beautiful has passed.

What the music is saying

Afterglow closes the album. Two sets of footprints lead toward the water as the sky fades and the first star appears. Heartfelt, soft and sweet, it is the feeling that stays after the music stops.

Style: Cinematic Instrumental
Feel: Heartfelt, Reflective, Bittersweet

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
