The story behind the music

Whenever I hear this piece, I imagine traveling by caravan toward a mysterious destination.

The journey feels long, but never lonely. There is a sense that the traveler is moving toward a place that is simultaneously unknown and deeply familiar. To me, it is ultimately a song about belonging.

What the music is saying

Homeward began as a caravan carrying the listener to a mysterious place. It became a long, flowing journey home, like a voyaging canoe under full sail, following the stars toward land.

Style: Symphonic Ballad
Feel: Flowing, Hopeful, Reflective

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
