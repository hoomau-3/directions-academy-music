The story behind the music

This is the only jazz piece I ever truly embraced.

Inspired by the cool sophistication of Dave Brubeck's work while remaining grounded in a steady pulse, it is a piece that makes me want to tap my foot, nod my head, and simply enjoy the groove. Rather than challenge the listener, it invites them in.

What the music is saying

Easy Sway is the one jazz piece David truly loves to play, fashioned after the cool jazz of the Take Five era. Instead of odd timings and an abundance of blue notes, it rides a steady 4/4 backbone that lets the instruments play off one another. A finger snapper, a toe tapper, a head nodder.

Style: Jazz Fusion
Feel: Relaxed, Rhythmic, Effortlessly Cool

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
