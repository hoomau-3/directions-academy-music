The story behind the music

This piece feels like watching a sunrise.

It begins quietly with piano and strings before gradually expanding into a broad orchestral landscape carried by French horns and soaring melodies. Every time I listen to it, I am reminded of the promise contained in a new beginning. It is a song of hope, possibility, and wonder.

What the music is saying

First Light is wonder at the start of a day. Sunrise spills across the bay, the boats wait on still water, and the mountain catches the first glow.

Style: Orchestral Pop
Feel: Hopeful, Glorious, Uplifting

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
