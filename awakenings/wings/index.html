The story behind the music

This piece is built around an idea I have always loved: that individual instruments can elevate one another and create something greater together than any could accomplish alone.

As the arrangement grows from piano and strings into a broad cinematic statement, the music seems to lift itself into the air. To me, it is a celebration of imagination, cooperation, and transformation.

What the music is saying

Wings is about instruments elevating one another, two or more coming together to make a fuller sound and carry the listener to new heights of imagination. It suggests ascent without being grandiose, and a quiet transformation along the way.

Style: Orchestral Pop
Feel: Inspirational, Uplifting, Expansive

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
