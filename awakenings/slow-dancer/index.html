The story behind the music

When I listen to this piece, I picture a couple moving together across a dance floor without hurry or effort.

The music is romantic, but not in a dramatic sense. It is more about comfort, trust, and the quiet beauty of moving through life with someone who understands you completely.

What the music is saying

Slow Dancer is a couple gliding across the floor in perfect harmony, unhurried and dreamlike. Like two swans on still water, every movement answers the other.

Style: Film Score Inspired
Feel: Dreamlike, Romantic, Graceful

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
