The story behind the music

This piece always makes me feel as though I am gliding effortlessly above the waves as they roll toward the shore.

Carried by sweeping strings and warm orchestral textures, it speaks to freedom, possibility, and the exhilaration of seeing the world from a higher perspective.

What the music is saying

Soaring is pure uplift. A seabird rides the wind low over the surf, sweeping toward the shore, carried by something bigger than itself.

Style: Orchestral Instrumental
Feel: Uplifting, Sweeping, Liberating

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
