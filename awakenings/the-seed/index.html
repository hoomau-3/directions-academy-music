The story behind the music

This piece grew from a melody shared with me many years ago by a gifted songwriter friend named Tony.

One day he played a few bars and dismissed them as another unfinished idea among hundreds. I never forgot it. Years later, I used that small musical seed as the foundation for this composition. New melodies gradually emerge, take the lead, and then gracefully make way for others, yet that original idea remains woven throughout the entire piece.

To me, this song is a tribute to the way inspiration travels from one person to another and continues to grow long after the original moment has passed.

What the music is saying

The Seed began as Tony's Song. David's bandmate Tony once played him about eight bars of a melody in his music room and said he had hundreds of starts like it that went nowhere. David never forgot it. Years later he used that melody to begin this piece, adding a new lead instrument at regular intervals. Listen closely and the original melody is still there, deep under the layers.

Style: Orchestral Pop
