Awakenings

Nearly forty years ago, I went through a season of creativity that I still struggle to explain.

I had no formal music training. I learned to play by watching my mother, listening carefully, and trusting my ear. Like many musicians in Hawaiʻi, I played what I felt more than what I could read on a page.

Then something changed.

For a period of time, melodies seemed to arrive from somewhere beyond my normal understanding. One idea would barely be finished before another appeared. I worked as quickly as I could, often giving pieces temporary names simply so I could save them and move on to the next tune waiting to be heard.

I call this collection Awakenings because that is what the experience felt like. It was as though I had awakened to music I had previously been deaf to.

Revisiting these songs decades later has been an unexpected journey. The original titles were often placeholders, but the music remained. By listening again and again, I began hearing the deeper stories hidden beneath the notes. What emerged was more than a collection of instrumental pieces. Together they form an emotional journey through wonder, freedom, imagination, connection, belonging, gratitude, and reflection.

These songs are not technical exercises. They are stories told through melody and feeling. Some soar above oceans and distant horizons. Others wander homeward through mysterious landscapes. Some celebrate friendship, inspiration, partnership, and love. All of them invite listeners to discover their own meaning within the music.

Written by ear, guided by intuition, and rediscovered decades later, Awakenings is both a return to the past and a conversation with it. These are the sounds of a time when music seemed to arrive faster than I could understand it, and the story of what those melodies revealed when I finally stopped long enough to listen.

Before Awakenings, a song

Before these instrumental pieces existed, there was a song.

"Into The Night" was the last song I recorded with my group, The Country Rebels, and the first step toward everything that followed. It now has its own home in Because of You, my album of thank-yous to the people who shaped me.
