The story behind the music

This piece began with a meaningless working title, but after years of listening, its true identity gradually revealed itself.

To me, it feels like saying goodbye with gratitude rather than sadness. The music begins with yearning and reflection before opening into something larger. It speaks of acceptance, connection, and becoming one with something beyond ourselves.

What the music is saying

Communion is about release into something larger than oneself. Two streams coming down from the high ground meet and become one. The music yearns, then lets go, and finds peace in becoming part of everything.

Style: Symphonic Pop, Classical Crossover
Feel: Transcendent, Reflective, Yearning

An instrumental. Music by David, first composed in the early 1990s on three MIDI keyboards and a Macintosh SE. Arrangement and creative partnership with AI.
