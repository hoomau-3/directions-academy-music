The story behind the music

The steady drum pulse and breath-like melodies create a feeling that is both ancient and timeless.

Whenever I hear it, I feel connected to something larger than myself. It is a piece filled with wonder, reflection, and quiet reverence for the stories carried through generations and remembered by the wind.

What the music is saying

The Wind Remembers is wonder and reflection. The drum keeps the heartbeat, the flute carries the memory, and the wind across the high ridge seems to hold every story it has ever passed.

Style: Native-Inspired Contemporary Instrumental
Feel: Wonder, Reflection, Serenity
